How Service Pricing Works
  • Prices set here are automatically applied when a guest requests a service from their dashboard.
  • The service charge is immediately added to their bill along with GST recalculation.
  • Services set to ₹0 are marked as Complimentary and shown to guests at no charge.
  • Disabled services are hidden from guests and cannot be requested.
  • Only the admin can change these prices — guests cannot modify service charges.
