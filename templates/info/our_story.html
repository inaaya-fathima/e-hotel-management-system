A Century of Extraordinary Hospitality

E-Hotel was born from a single vision — to create a place where guests felt not merely accommodated, but genuinely celebrated. Founded in 1924 by the Mehta family in the heart of Jaipur, our first property was a converted royal haveli that blended Rajasthani grandeur with modern comfort.

Over a century, we have grown from one heritage property into a chain spanning six of India's most iconic destinations. Through every decade, our philosophy has remained unchanged: every guest deserves the very best.

Today, E-Hotel stands as one of India's most celebrated luxury hospitality brands — honoured with numerous awards, loved by millions of guests, and home to a family of over 500 dedicated hospitality professionals.
