Luxury That Loves the Planet

At E-Hotel, we believe the finest luxury must leave the world better than it found it. Our Green Excellence programme, launched in 2019, commits every property to measurable environmental targets that we report on annually.

  • 70% renewable energy across all properties by 2025
  • Zero single-use plastics since 2021
  • Rainwater harvesting at all properties
  • 10,000 trees planted annually in partnership with local communities
  • 40% locally-sourced ingredients in all restaurants
  • Electric vehicle fleet for guest transfers
